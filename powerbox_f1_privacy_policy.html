Powerbox F1 – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Powerbox F1 is a companion application for compatible Powerbox BT hardware used with the Renault Twizy. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information handled by the app

Powerbox F1 uses Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to communicate with a nearby Powerbox BT device. The app may display vehicle, battery and device information received from the hardware, and send configuration commands requested by the user. This communication is intended to take place locally between the phone and the Powerbox BT device.

Personal data collection

Powerbox F1 does not require users to create an account. The developer does not intentionally collect personal information, user profiles, or app-usage analytics through the app. Information shown from the connected Powerbox BT device is not intentionally uploaded to the developer's servers.

Internet access and updates

The app may connect to an update server to check for and download app or Powerbox BT firmware updates. These requests are used to deliver updates. Like most web servers, the hosting provider may process technical connection information, such as IP addresses, to deliver requests and maintain security. Any server-side retention or logging must be checked against the actual hosting configuration.

Third-party services

The app may rely on platform or software components to provide Bluetooth and other functionality. The developer should verify whether any included third-party SDK collects data independently before publishing this policy.

Children's privacy

The app is designed to operate compatible vehicle hardware and is not specifically directed at children.
